# The Tech Hour Podcast

Weekly tech podcast with professional audio mixing and sound design.

## Production Workflow

Every episode goes through:

1. **Recording** - Clean vocal tracks with proper gain staging
2. **Editing** - Removing pauses and mistakes
3. **Mixing** - Balancing levels, adding music
4. **Mastering** - Final polish for broadcast quality

## Equipment Used

- Microphone: Shure SM7B
- Interface: Universal Audio Apollo
- DAW: Ableton Live 11
